Every profile is its own device

A profile has its own cookies and its own identity: screen, graphics card, fonts, time zone, fingerprints. Make your first one.

Devices

Identities read from real devices. A profile made from one presents itself as that model of device — its screen, graphics card and its limits, fonts and voices — with a noise seed of its own.

Reading another device

Open the collector page on that device — send it the file collect.html from the manager's ui folder, or host that file on any HTTPS site — in Chrome, or in Safari on an iPhone. Press “Read this device”, download the result, and import it here.
